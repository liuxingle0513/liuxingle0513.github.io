Shenzhen, China

Research Assistant

Control and Decision Lab, CUHK-Shenzhen

Supervisors: Prof. Xiaoqiang Ji & Dr. Zike Yuan

Vision-language navigationEmbodied AILLM RL & ILDiffusion policyEdge deployment
